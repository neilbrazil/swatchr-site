Privacy Policy

Last updated 6 August 2026

Swatchr: Color Picker does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. There is no account, no sign-in, no backend, and no data sync of any kind. The app has no way to identify you.

What the app does on its own: nothing leaves your device. Camera frames and photos you pick a color from are processed entirely on-device and are never uploaded, stored, or shared. Saved palettes and widget data live locally in the app's own container. There is no analytics SDK and no advertising SDK anywhere in the app.

Camera and photo access

  • Camera. Used only to sample the color under the on-screen crosshair, live, as you move it. Camera frames are read for that single pixel value and discarded immediately — Swatchr never records, saves, or transmits camera video or images.
  • Photo library. When you pick a photo, Swatchr reads the color at the point you tap. The photo itself is never copied, uploaded, or stored by the app beyond what's needed to show it on screen while you're picking.

What stays on your device

  • Colors you've picked and saved. Your saved palette, the last color shown in the widget, and any exported color cards are stored locally and never leave your device.
  • Usage events (app opened, a color picked, a format copied, a palette saved, purchase completed). These are written to Apple's local system log (OSLog) for on-device debugging only — they are never sent anywhere, not even to us.

The one-time purchase

Swatchr offers a single one-time in-app purchase to unlock the saved palette, widget, and export card. Purchases are handled by Apple's StoreKit and by RevenueCat, the third-party service Swatchr uses to validate that purchase and keep the unlock working if you reinstall or move to a new device.

To do that, RevenueCat receives an anonymous, app-specific identifier (not your Apple ID or name) plus transaction data from Apple — product purchased, price, and date. It does not receive your photos, camera data, saved colors, or anything else you enter in the app. RevenueCat's own privacy policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Payment itself is processed entirely by Apple. Swatchr never sees your card details, billing address, or Apple ID — that transaction is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

What we receive

Nothing directly. If you download Swatchr from the App Store, Apple may share aggregate statistics such as download counts and territory with us as the developer — that data comes from Apple and is not personally identifying. If you email us for support, we receive whatever you put in that email.

Your data, and removing it

Because nothing is collected or transmitted, there is no account to close and no data held by us to access, correct, export, or delete. Everything the app has saved lives in its container on your device — deleting the app removes it, including any saved palette and widget data.

Children

Swatchr is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Swatchr ever needs to collect or transmit data — it has no plans to — this policy will be updated before that version ships.
