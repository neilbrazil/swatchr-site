Swatchr: Color Picker

Pick any color from your camera or photos. Copy hex, RGB, HSL, and CMYK in one tap.

Download on the App Store

Key features

  • Live camera picker Drag the crosshair over anything in view and watch the color sample update live.
  • Pick from photos Tap any point in a saved photo to lift its exact color.
  • Every format, one tap Hex, RGB, HSL, and CMYK are all shown at once — tap any of them to copy.
  • Save, widget, export Unlock an unlimited saved palette, a Home Screen widget for your last color, and shareable export cards.
